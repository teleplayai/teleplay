# Teleplay feedback

[Teleplay](https://www.teleplay.ai) is an AI game maker: you describe a game in one sentence, a bot play-tests it, and you play it in your browser, alone or with friends by link.

This is where you tell us what's broken and what you'd love to see next. Teleplay's own code isn't here, and this isn't the place for account or billing questions.

## Report a bug

**[Open a bug report →](../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)**

What helps us fix it fast:

1. what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
2. the game's link, if the game is public or shared by link;
3. your browser and device (for example *Chrome on Windows*, *Safari on iPhone*).

## Suggest an idea

**[Share an idea →](../../issues/new?template=idea.yml)**

One idea per issue, please. If someone already suggested it, add a 👍 to theirs instead: that's how we see what people want most.

## What happens next

- Every issue gets a label: `bug`, `idea`, `multiplayer`, `3d` or `editor`.
- When something is fixed or shipped, we close the issue and say so.

## Please don't post

- personal information: your email, real name, or anything private;
- someone else's game without their permission;
- security problems: email **info@teleplay.ai** instead ([SECURITY](https://github.com/teleplayai/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md)).
